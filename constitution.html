DefinitionsDefinition of terms and acronyms

Definition of Terms

In this constitution unless the context indicates otherwise,

a) Academic year – Means Define Calendar years consisting of two semesters for the schools of CHD, Agribusiness and Education, but three trimesters for school of Health sciences.

b) University Council Means Great Lakes University of Kisumu Council as constituted in terms of the Great Lakes University of Kisumu governance structure and Statutes.

c) The University is a reference to Great Lakes University of Kisumu as established by the laws of Kenya in the current state with existing campuses and for additional ones to be established in future;

d) Student(s) are references to a person(s) Full Time or Part Time by valid registration in the University for Purposes of obtaining a qualification of the University or any other person who is determined by the Senate to be a student;

e) Dean of Students is any person recognized by the university Council and Senate to be in charge of Students' affairs at the university;

f) Working Day is a reference to a day in the week except any day that falls on the Saturday and/or Sunday or any day that is declared a National Public Holiday.

g) Calendar Day is a reference to a day in the week including weekends and public holidays

h) Office Bearer is a reference to any persons(s) elected or appointed to hold a position of authority and responsibility as provided in this constitution.

i) bona fide student: Any student who has met the registration requirements in line with the current trimester.

j) Deciding Vote - Is a vote casted in the House of congress to break a tie.

k) Disability – Means any physical, Mental, sensory condition or illness that has, or is perceived by the significant sectors of the community to have substantial or long-term effect on an individual's ability to carry out ordinary day to day activities as defined under article 260 of the constitution of the Republic of Kenya.

l) International Students – Is non-Kenyan Students with relevant documents, affirming that he/she is from the said country.

m) Students Barasa – Is a consultative meeting by GLUKSA Members occasioned by an emergency.

n) Semester Registration: Refers to a situation whereby a student has reported, registered for units.

o) Nominated Members of Congress – these are bona fide members of GLUKSA appointed by the L.E.C in line with chapter 7 article 41 of this constitution into the Congress.

p) Social welfare - system of programs, services, and interventions designed to prove the well being of individuals and social groups, particularly the vulnerable, disadvantaged or marginalized groups in society

q) Moribund - To lack value/vitality or activity/ in terminal decline

Chapter 1Students' Association and supremacy of this constitution

Article 1. Establishment

a) We, the students of Great Lakes University of Kisumu hereby declare that there shall, for all practical purposes, be established at GLUK a students' Association to be known as "GREAT LAKES UNIVERSTY OF KISUMU STUDENTS' ASSOCIATION" abbreviated in style of "GLUKSA", to act and function in accordance with the provision of this constitution and within the universities Act 2024, the Great Lakes University statutes 2012, the relevant and applicable laws and university policies.

b) The Headquarters of GLUKSA shall be housed in an office situated in the main campus of Great Lakes University of Kisumu P.O BOX 2224-40100 Kisumu.

c) There shall be created in each constituent colleges and affiliated institutions BRANCH offices that shall be accountable to GLUKSA members in those branches. Branch offices shall operate within the confine provided in this constitution

d) Jurisdiction: GLUKSA shall take a federal system of government with each campus based on physical geographical setting as shall be created from time to time, the university Council and in particular: -

i. GLUK Main campus

ii. GLUK Milimani CORC.

iii. GLUK Nairobi CORC

iv. Any other campuses and CORC as may be established by the university council

Article 2. Supremacy of this constitution

a) This constitution is the supreme law of GLUKSA and binds all members and GLUKSA organs to work towards the good of the University and all students.

b) No person may claim or exercise authority except as authorized under this constitution.

c) Every member of GLUKSA has an obligation to respect uphold and defend this constitution GLUKSA constitution shall be in compliance with universities (Amendment) act 2016

Article 3. Values and Principles

a) The values and principles include the rule of law, democracy and participation of members', inclusiveness, discrimination, good governance, integrity, transparency and accountability.

b) The values herein, shall be binding to all members and GLUKSA organs whenever any of them;

i) Applies or interprets this constitution

ii) Makes or implements decision

c) GLUKSA shall in its operations be guided by the following values and principles

i) National Unity

ii) good governance

iii) Accountability

iv) Gender equity

v) Inclusivity and diversity

vi) Collective and responsible student leadership

vii) Commitment to academic excellence

Article 4. Aims and objectives

The aims and objectives of GLUKSA shall be:

i. To establish friendly relations and understanding with other relevant organization whose objectives are to promote students' welfare (provided ALWAYS) that such cooperation is not determined to the university and the country.

ii. To foster peaceful enjoyment of the freedom rights and privileges provided for in this constitution and in the university

iii. To promote students' welfare and other useful activities

iv. To promote platform through which students can freely express their views, thoughts, expression and creative talents.

v. To promote unity and understanding among students, between students and the university administration, members of the staff and the general public

Article 5. Organs of GLUKSA

The organs of GLUKSA shall be: -

I. The Annual general meeting – herein referred to as the AGM

II. Students' Governing council herein referred to as S. GC; a) SLC b) SEC

III. Special general meeting herein referred to as SGM

IV. Students Congress

V. Any other organs determined by Students' Governing Council subject to the approval of the Congress

Chapter 2Membership

Article 6. Membership of GLUKSA

a) It shall be all student admitted to the university for a course leading to qualification of an award of short Courses, certificate, diploma, degree, Masters or a PhD of the university and is also bona fide.

Article 7. Cessation of membership

a) A member of GLUKSA shall cease to be so only upon Graduation at the university or when such member ceases to be a student at the university through the following ways

i) Suspension and /or expulsion from either GLUKSA or GLUK

ii) Death

iii) If one is not bona fide

iv) Deferment

v) Inter-University transfer

b) Any member who ceases to be a GLUKSA member shall not be entitled to any refund of subscription fee or any other monetary contributions by him/her.

Chapter 3Rights and obligations of members

Article 8. Rights of Members

Subject to Chapter 4 of the Constitution of Kenya 2010;

a) Application

i) The Bill of Rights bind all students and the University. As such, all the aforementioned parties have the duty to respect, protect and promote these rights.

ii) The student organs constituted by this Constitution in Chapter 1 must take reasonable steps to ensure the protection and promotion of these rights, including through engagement, where necessary with the University authorities.

b) Equality

No student shall be unfairly discriminated against nor denied fair treatment and access to justice on the basis of gender, religion, creed, tribe, color, disability and nationality.

c) Human dignity

Every student has inherent human dignity and the right to have his or her dignity respected and protected.

d) Confidentiality of student records

Every student has the right to the confidentiality of his or her university records except when required by parents, guardian or financial sponsor.

e) Student success

i) Every student has the right to an enabling environment in which student success and academic excellence are encouraged and pursued.

ii) Every student has the right to quality education, academic support, and transparent and justifiable assessment procedures.

iii) The bodies constituted by this Constitution in Chapter 4 have a duty to identify and work towards the eradication of barriers to the exercise of these rights.

f) Freedom of religion, belief and opinion

Every student has the right to freedom of religion, belief and opinion.

g) Freedom of expression

Every student has the right to freedom of expression, which does not extend to hate speech, violence, slender, propaganda, and cybercrime which includes –

(i) Freedom of academic expression and scientific research.

(ii) Freedom to receive and impart information and ideas.

h) Assembly, demonstration and petition

Every student has the right, peacefully and unarmed, to assemble and demonstrate on campus, and to present petitions to GLUKSA organs and to the University management upon prior notification of seven working days to the Dean of students.

i) Freedom of association

Every student has the right to freedom of association on campus, including the right to form any student group, association or society; as long as such associations are in line with the GLUKSA constitution, University governing rules and regulations and the constitution of Kenya.

j) Freedom of Fair hearing

Every member has a right to freedom of fair hearing and enquiry.

K) Administrative action

Every student whose rights or legitimate expectations are materially and adversely affected by any decision taken by GLUKSA, a member of a student body, or the university body, has the right to; -

(i) Be notified of the nature and purpose of the proposed action;

(ii) A reasonable opportunity to make representations;

(iii) Adequate notice of any right of review or internal appeal, where applicable; and

(iv) Request reasons for the decision and to be furnished with written reasons within a reasonable time.

L) Limitation

i) The rights in this chapter may only be limited in terms of legal rules of general application, which, for purposes of this section, are deemed to include University regulations, rules and policies.

ii) Limitations must be designed to achieve objectives that are consistent with the values of an open democratic community based on human dignity, equality and freedom.

iii) Limitations may not limit the rights in this chapter more than necessary, and the impact they have on the rights of students must be proportionate to their objectives.

Article 9. Enforcement

Any student, GLUKSA or any group of students, whether acting in their own interest or in the interest of a group or class of students, may approach the office of the Dean of Students for appropriate relief in the event of an alleged violation of their rights under this Constitution.

Article 10. Interpretation

a) The Bill of Student Rights must be interpreted in conformity with chapter 4 of the Constitution of the Republic of Kenya, the Higher Education Act, the Statute of Great Lakes University of Kisumu and University policy and regulations.

b) Every member of GLUKSA has a right to access services and facilities offered by GLUKSA.

c) Every member of GLUKSA except members appointed to the Electoral Commission of GLUKSA or Elections Appeal committee shall have a right to vie for any positions declared vacant.

d) Every member shall be entitled to vote for every contestable post conducted as per the provisions of this constitution. Members of the Electoral commission shall vote just before voting begins on the material day.

e) Every member of GLUKSA has a right to access to all relevant information relating to the affairs of the Organization including to inspect the books of account of GLUKSA and records of account, audit reports after the elapse of seven working days written notice to the finance secretary.

f) Every member of GLUKSA is entitled to access a copy of this constitution. The copies shall be supplied in electronic form and some printed for students' leaders, for University Libraries, the Dean of students' office and any other places as shall be found appropriate.

g) Every ordinary member shall have the right to requisition for a vote to be taken in the AGM. All GLUKSA members are entitled to diversified entertainment activity.

Article 11. Obligations of Members

a) Every member shall promote objectives of GLUKSA as provided under CHAPTER 1 of this Constitution and in particular take part in all the activities of the students in the Association.

b) Every member shall respect GLUKSA including all legal decisions and actions taken by its organs as established in this constitution.

c) Every member shall respect property owned by GLUKSA, the University and the Public. Any member who with malice causes damage to property owned by GLUKSA, the university or the public shall be subjected to disciplinary provisions of GLUKSA.

d) Every member shall be required to honor individual financial obligation to GLUKSA, including and in particular, pay initial registration and subsequent subscription as determined from time to time by the students' governing council subject to approval by the students' Assembly and the university Senate.

Chapter 4Students' Governing Council

Article 12. Establishment of GLUKSA Governing Council

i. There shall be student governing council of GLUKSA and as per the provision of this constitution and universities' Amendment Act 2016

ii. The students' council shall be the overall coordinating organ of the organization

iii. The office of the students' council shall be located at the main campus of Great Lakes university.

Article 13. Executive Authority and responsibilities of the student Governing council

a) The executive authority of GLUKSA is vested in the students' Governing council and as well as the laws of the Republic of Kenya, universities Act 2024 and Great lakes university charter 2012 statutes and policies.

b) And as well as any relevant by laws that will emerge as amendments to this constitution by;

i) Ensuring that all members uphold this constitution and that the constitution is free from willful interference

ii) Initiating policies and or-by laws and carrying them and with approval of the students' Assembly for proper running and operating of GLUKSA and its organs

iii) Managing or appointing people to manage GLUKSA assets

iv) Keeping accurate and up to date records of GLUKSA

v) Implementing resolutions made by students' Assembly

vi) Coordinate GLUKSA operation and activities that are common in all campuses and community outreach Centre

vii) Summon meetings with a resolution supported by not less than two thirds of its members

viii) The GLUKSA council shall be responsible for furtherance and achieving the objectives of the organization as provided for this constitution.

ix) With good reason(s) have powers to appoint an interim committee for any named task; the committee shall stand dissolved upon the completion of the task.

Article 16. Liability of the Students' Governing council

a) The members of the Governing Council, may be held jointly or severally liable for negligence and mismanagement of the affairs of GLUKSA but in any event shall not be held liable for the misdeeds of individual members in their private capacity nor suffer victimization in the discharge of their responsibilities Provided that they shall carry responsibly with dignity and without misuse of their various offices.

b) An official in the student governing council shall hold office for a term of one year and maybe eligible for reelection for one last term.

c) A person who has held an office as member of the STUDENTS' COUNCIL for a similar Association of a University or constituent college in Kenya for two terms is INELIGIBLE/DISQUALIFIED to be elected as a COUNCIL MEMBER of GLUKSA.

Article 17. Vacation of office

a. A member of the SGC shall vacate the office;

i. If such office bearer has ceased to be student in the university or such a member has completed studies at the university or suspended from the university

ii. When a new office bearer is elected and inaugurated in accordance with the provision of this constitution.

iii. If such office bearer is convicted of a criminal offence by a competent court of law

iv. If such a person by reason of infirmity of mind or body is unable to execute the function of his /her office. If the such a person holds the office of the chair, his/her vice assumes office and he/she nominates/appoints a new vice whom shall be;

a) of the opposite gender.

b) If he/she contested for the seat would qualify for the same in accordance to the provision of this constitution.

v. If members decide in accordance to this constitution that they have no confidence in such member being an office bearer

vi. If such office bearer resigns and personally hands in letter of resignation to the speaker of the students' Assembly giving a seven working days' notice and reason (s) for resignation.

b. In the event of vacancy

In the event of vacancy except in the circumstance when on a new office bearer is elected and inaugurated in accordance with the provision of this constitution

i. If the vacancy is created within 60 days to the next general election and the position has no deputy, the SGC shall appoint another person from among members of the Congress to act and serve in the vacancy created until the next General election. And shall enjoy the benefits of the offices.

ii. If the vacancy is created more than 60 days before the general Election a by-election should be created

c) Vote of No confidence on an office bearer

i. Any motion proposing a vote of no confidence in a member of the SGC shall originate from the member of the Congress who shall table it in the assembly after a written notice of not less than fourteen days.

Motion mover shall;

a) Collect two thirds' signatures of members of the congress.

b) Table the motion to the house of Congress.

ii. The speaker shall write to the Secretary General on the motion and allegations, unless the S.G is the victim, then the speaker shall write to the vice-chair.

iii. The speaker shall send the delegates to the public to collect views within seven days then bring their findings to the Congress.

iv. if according to the public views they agree, the speaker shall pose a question to the congress if the impeachment Motion should proceed in a plenary way or through the committee. If its through plenary the member shall be summoned in the Congress and if committee way, then they will debate, give findings and their final vadict.

v. if the leader is impeached, the speaker notifies the Secretary General who will there after notify the I.E.C.

vi. An elected Leader may be impeached because of the following reasons;

a) Blatant violation of the provision of this constitution

b) Gross misconduct

d) financial mismanagement

e) Any other reason that would render the person unsuitable to be in office

- The IEC shall be given 30days to organize for another election

- Once impeached, a members shall not hold any GLUKSA office

a. If by any given chance an elected member of SGC fails completely to do his/her duties, develops criminal behaviors, become disrespectful to the entire SGC, undermines the authority /duty of another office, misses SGC meeting for more than three consecutive times or/and behaves in any other way that is detrimental to the student's organization leadership then the following action will be taken; Suspension shall take place not less than a month and not more than two Months. Chapter 5Meetings

Article 19. Meeting of Student Governing Council (SGC)

- This Meeting shall consist of members of S.E.C and S.L.C

a) For effectiveness the SGC shall be required to meet

I. The Governing council shall meet at least four times each semester.

II. The meeting shall be presided over by; a. The chairperson; b. In the absence of the chairperson the vice chairperson.

III. Every meeting of the Governing council shall be documented by the Secretary General in a manner resolved by the said council in its first meeting following inauguration to office

IV. The quorum for the commencement and conducts of a meeting of the Governing council shall be half plus one of all its members.

b) Decision in the Students' Governing council

I. Any question proposed for the decision in a meeting of the Governing Council shall be determined by the majority of votes of the members present and voting.

II. On any question proposed for the decision, the person presiding over the meeting shall: -

a) If the chairperson will have a casting vote but not an original vote; or

b) If not the chairperson, will both have the original and casting vote.

c) If there is a tie, the chairperson shall have the deciding votes.

Article 20. Annual General Meeting (A.G.M)

i. All GLUKSA members shall have the right to attend the AGM

ii. The AGM shall normally take place during the 2nd semester before General elections within twenty-eight days from the commencement of that semester to: -

a) Receive all reports from all outgoing SGC Members

b) Approve the minutes of the last AGM as presented by the Secretary General

c) Receive the annual financial report from the outgoing Treasurer

d) Receive an overall report of GLUKSA operation from the outgoing chairperson

e) Discuss any other business relevant to finances of GLUKSA

iii. AGM shall be called through a notice given at least fourteen days before the meeting

Chapter 6Congress

Article 22. Establishment

There is established the students Congress of GLUKSA.

The Students Congress shall, Subject to the provisions of this constitution be the Legislative Organ of GLUKSA.

The legislative power of GLUKSA shall be vested in the students Congress which shall operate according to the provisions of this Constitution.

ii. Ex. Officio members of the Congress shall be; a) The Dean of Students; b) The speaker of the students Congress; c) Vice chairperson who shall represent interest of the student's executive council (SEC); d) Sergeant at Arms; nominated by the house business committee at every sitting

b) The person(s) to be nominated shall be person(s) who, if they had been nominated for the Congress elections, would qualify to be elected as members of the Congress.

c) The S.E.C shall nominate Members to the students' Congress and shall present the names of the nominated Members to the chairperson independent electoral Commission within seven days after inauguration for publication.

d) All GLUKSA members are recognized as belonging to certain general constituencies in the Students Congress.

Article 24. Dean of students as a member of Students Congress

The dean of students shall be an ex-official Member of the Congress but shall not be entitled to vote on any question before the Congress

The Dean of Students shall: -

i) Preside over the swearing in of the speaker and his/her deputy, Clerk and other Members of the Congress in the presence of the Students Legal Advisor.

ii) Be an ex-officio member of Congress.

iii) Oversee investigation of any official of GLUKSA found to have violated any of this constitution.

Article 25. Recall and Vacancies in the Congress

a) At any time, Members of a given constituency may move a vote to recall their representatives of the Congress, which shall be passed through a resolution supported by not less than two – thirds of the registered Members of the said constituency at that time.

The office of a member of the Congress shall be vacant if: -

i) The member resigns in writing to the speaker of the Congress.

ii) The member of the Congress is recalled in accordance with sub-section (a) here above

iii) By reason of infirmity of mind or body, such member is unable to execute the function of the office.

iv) The member is absent for Two consecutive sitting without the permission of the speaker.

v) The Congress is dissolved in accordance with the provision of this constitution

vi) Circumstances that would have disqualified that member from election arise.

i. Taking minutes of congress proceedings

ii. Tallying votes in the congress

iii. Keep true accurate and up to date records of the proceedings of the congress

iv. Receive record and forward all motion to the speaker.

Role of constinuency representatives

i. Representation

ii. Legislation

Article 27. Functions of the congress

a) The students' assembly shall exercise authority and have responsibility as provided in the constitution and in particular shall: -

i. Be the legislative organ of GLUKSA

ii. Debate and approve GLUKSA policies, proposals, & decisions as tabled by committees

iii. Monitor Implementation of its resolution by the SGC

vi. Debate, approve and adopt budget proposals from the SGC

i. Through its committees Institute a probe into any suspected breaches of the GLUKSA constitution, by –laws and policies

ii. Provide directions on any emergency matters affecting the students body after receiving due report from the S.G.C

iii. Subject to section 16 (a and b) of this constitution, have power to impeach and pass a vote of no confidence in any Member.

b) The Congress shall hear and discuss the state of GLUKSA as presented by the chairperson in its first sitting each semester.

c) The students Assembly shall NOT, by rule, vest executive authority by itself.

The inauguration of the newly elected members of Congress shall take place within twenty-one (21) days after the inauguration of the S.G.C

However, the members of the Congress shall be inaugurated jointly as a group.

Article 29. Regulations and Procedures in the Congress

a) The Congress shall subject to this constitution: -

i. Make rules to regulate its own procedures including, in particular rules for orderly conduct of proceedings

ii. Subject to any rule made under sub-section (a) (i) above, regulate the procedure of any of its committees

iii. Subject to this constitution, the Congress shall act not withstanding a vacancy in its membership (Including a vacancy not filled within the Congress first meets after a general election), and the presence of participate in the proceedings of the Congress, shall not invalidate those proceedings.

b) Without prejudice to the powers conferred by subsection a (i) and (ii) above, the Congress may, for the purpose of orderly and effective discharge of its business, provide for the powers, privileges and humanities of the Assembly to its committees and members.

c) The Congress shall also table bills for discussion:

i). First reading – A bill is introduced in the assembly and it is immediately assigned to a committee for consideration

ii) Second reading – The draft of a bill is read in a second time, a vote is taken in the general outlines of the bill and it is sent to the designated committee

iii) Third reading – It is when a bill is read with all amendments and given final approval by the Congress.

Subject to this constitution, the first sitting of the Congress shall be held at such a place that the S.E.C may determine from time to time, within each campus. Thereafter, the speaker, in consultation with the Vice chairperson shall convene the subsequent meetings.

The Congress shall stand dissolved together with the students governing council.

i. The speaker elected to serve for one calendar year.

Have no voting right to matters of the house

i) If he/she is the speaker, have a deciding vote not an original vote.

Chapter 9Elections

Article 60. General Principles

1. The election process shall be subject to the provision of this constitution

2. The electoral system of GLUKSA shall be based on the right of every member to vote by standing for election and holding office in a manner that allows for practical and effective functioning of GLUKSA.

3. Elections shall be free and fair

a) Free from violence, intimidation, improper influence or corruption;

b) Conducted by independent body;

c) Transparent

d) Administered in an impartial, neutral, official, accurate and accountable manner.

4. Elections shall be conducted through secret ballot by the Electoral College

5. For purpose of conducting elections; All GLUKSA members shall be constituted into electoral colleges on academic departments which in this constitution shall be academic programs determined from time to time, making the GLUKSA constituencies.

6. Student/ member from each Electoral College constituted in 5 above shall elect three delegates;

a) From among candidates who are not contestant for the position of the student council.

b) Of who not more than two-third shall be of the same gender

c) Have no pending disciplinary case and

d) Be in session

Has not served under any disciplinary suspension from GLUKSA and/or the university unless such a person provides a letter reinstating him/her back as a student.

7. In the event that the three elected delegates are of the same gender; the next best candidate of the opposite gender shall take place of the third-place person – unless the department has students of one gender.

8. The independent Electoral commission of GLUKSA shall have powers to formulate rules and regulations for the proper conduct of election

9. The outcome of the election shall reflect;

a) Not more than two-third of the members of the members of the GLUKSA council be of same gender

b) National diversity

c) Fair representation of persons with disabilities

10. Every student council shall be elected in accordance with the universities Act 2016

11. Electoral college representatives, delegates, once elected and upon conclusion of the GLUKSA council shall serve for a period of one calendar year as elected members of students' Assembly

12. Any member of the organization who has served as a GLUKSA council member is not eligible to vie as an Electoral College member.

13) If some delegates aspirants are disqualified, the position shall be re-advertised.

14. A candidate running for the position of chairperson shall pick a running mate of opposite gender and the two shall be elected as one ticket.

15) Every department must have representation in the congress

Article 72. Election Offences

Any GLUKSA member is subject for disqualification from election they shall be found guilty of the following;

Any candidate aspiring for any office in GLUKSA, and starts campaigning before end of AGM.

An election offence shall disqualify a candidate.

b) For purposes of this section, an election offence shall include;

i. Rigging – Illegal interference with the process of an election by increasing the vote share of a candidate in favor of the rival candidate

ii. Use of force and violence during election period, is when a person, directly or indirectly, in person or by any other person, on his behalf, inflicts or threatens to inflict injury, damage, harm or loss on/or against a person – so as to induce or compel that person to induce or compel that person to support a particular person

iii. Giving false information to the electoral commission

iv. Campaigning on the polling day and or any act/omission which defect the tenets of free and fair election

v. Mounting campaign posters for any candidate outside places designated for such posters;

vi. Foul language and hate speech – When a person interferes with a free election process by canvassing and campaigning using language which is threatening, abusive or insulting against a candidate.

vii. Slur based ethnicity, religion, gender. - When a person interferes with a free election process by canvassing and campaigning using language which is threatening or engages in any kind of action which may advocate hatred or influence the voters on grounds of ethnicity, race, religion, gender or any other ground of discrimination

viii. Bribery – A person who during an election period – directly or indirectly offers a bribe to influence a voter in any manner unlawfully, influences the results of an election

Section 4: Tendering Procedure

1. Initiation: A request for procurement shall originate from the relevant GLUKSA docket and be approved by the GLUKSA Executive Council, confirming budget allocation.

2. Tender Invitation: The Committee shall prepare and issue a clear invitation to tender/request for quotations, specifying the scope of work, technical specifications, evaluation criteria, and closing date.

3. Tender Opening: Tenders shall be opened publicly at the stipulated time and venue, and the names of all bidders and their prices shall be recorded.

4. Evaluation and Award: The Committee shall evaluate the tenders based on the pre-determined criteria, which shall include:

5. Compliance with technical specifications.

* Price and value for money.

* Experience and capacity of the bidder.

* Delivery timelines.

* A formal report recommending the successful bidder shall be prepared and signed by all members of the Committee present.

6. Contracting: The Chairperson Finance secretary and Secretary General shall sign the contract on behalf of GLUKSA, following the award decision by the Committee and subject to the approval of the Executive Council.

Procurement and Tendering Process

Section 1: Establishment of the Tendering Committee

Establishment: There shall be established a GLUKSA Tendering and Procurement Committee (herein referred to as "the Committee") which shall be responsible for overseeing the procurement of goods, services, and works on behalf of the Association.

Mandate: The Committee shall ensure that all procurement activities are conducted in a fair, transparent, competitive, and cost-effective manner, adhering to the principles of prudent financial management and this Constitution.

Section 2: Composition of the Tendering Committee

The Committee shall be comprised of the following five (5) members, serving ex-officio by virtue of their office:

1. The Chairperson of GLUKSA (or their duly appointed representative from the Executive Council), who shall serve as the Chairperson of the Committee.

2. The Secretary General of GLUKSA, who shall serve as the Secretary to the Committee.

3. The finance minister (or Treasurer) of GLUKSA, who shall be responsible for providing budget-related advice.

4. The Chairperson of the Concerned Docket/Committee under whose mandate the goods or services fall.

5. One (1) Representative from the GLUKSA Congress/Assembly, elected by the Congress/Assembly, to ensure legislative oversight.

Note: A simple majority of the Committee members shall form a quorum for any meeting.

Section 3: General Tendering and Procurement Principles

Prudence and Accountability: All procurement shall be necessary and budgeted for, demonstrating the best value for GLUKSA funds.

1. Transparency: All tender invitations, procedures, and contract awards shall be clearly documented and communicated to the relevant stakeholders, as determined by the Committee.

Methods of Procurement: The Committee shall adopt appropriate procurement methods based on the value and nature of the goods/services, which may include:

1. Open Tendering: For high-value contracts.

2. Restricted Tendering: For specialized goods/services or when there is an urgent need.

3. Request for Quotations/Proposals: For low to medium-value purchases.

4. Conflict of Interest: No member of the Committee or any GLUKSA official shall participate in the tendering process or award of a contract in which they, their immediate family, or an affiliated business has a direct or indirect financial interest. Any such interest must be immediately declared.
