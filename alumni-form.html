🔐 Your information and your rights

What it is for: alumni engagement, mentorship, networking and communication about University activities, as the form says. It is never sold or used for advertising.

Who sees it: only GLUK's Directorate of External Affairs and the yearbook's super admin. Other students never see it, and nothing from it appears on the yearbook's pages. Everything the office opens or exports is recorded in an audit log.

Your rights (Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019): you can see your record here at any time and correct it yourself, and you can ask for it to be removed with Withdraw my alumni record. Deleting your yearbook profile does not delete this record: it is kept separately, because you agreed to it separately. Questions: bandadevelopers2026@gmail.com.
