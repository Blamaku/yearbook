Our university

Great Lakes University of Kisumu (GLUK) is a premier private chartered university located in Kisumu City, on the shores of Lake Victoria — heart of the Great Lakes region of East Africa.

Founded with a vision to produce graduates of integrity, competence and compassion, GLUK is committed to academic excellence and community transformation.

Your privacy: questions and answers

Straight answers about what the yearbook keeps, who can see it, and how to change or remove it.

What does the yearbook keep about me?

Only what you type into your profile and the photos you add. You fill in your name, registration number, school, programme and class year. Everything else (WhatsApp, email, birthday, bio, memories, photos) is optional, so leave out anything you would rather not share.

Signing in (with email and password, or with Google) is handled by Google's Firebase. The yearbook never sees or stores your password.

Who can see my profile?

Like a printed yearbook, your profile is public: anyone with the yearbook's link can see your name, photos, school, programme, class year and what you wrote (home county or country, the county you live in now, bio, hobbies, clubs, memories and what you are up to). Comments on profiles are public too.

Never public: your registration number, WhatsApp number, email, constituency, exact current location and birthday. Only you, the yearbook's admin and the university's Office of the Dean of Students can see your registration number.

Who can see my WhatsApp number and email?

Only students and staff who have their own yearbook profile, and only when they tap to open them. Each person can open at most 20 classmates' contacts a day, so nobody can collect everyone's numbers. Contacts are never shown on anonymous profiles, and you can leave them blank.

The university's Office of the Dean of Students can also see them in its student records, for student welfare and club records. Every list it opens and every export is recorded in an audit log the admin reviews.

Can I share less, or stay anonymous?

Yes. When you create your profile you can choose Stay anonymous, which shares only your name and class details. You can also open Edit My Profile at any time to remove photos or anything you wrote.

Anonymous profiles are left out of the Students feed, Faces of 2026, the graduation reel and the class superlatives.

Are my letters, time capsule, votes and friends private?
  • Letters to one graduate are read only by you and that graduate. A letter to your first-year self stays with you unless you choose to share it.
  • Time capsule notes stay sealed until your graduation day, and only you can open them.
  • Superlative votes are secret. The yearbook never shows who voted for whom; only the top 3 in each category are revealed.
  • Your friends list is private unless you choose to show it, and friend requests are never shown to anyone else.
Is my information sold, shared or used for ads?

No. The yearbook has no ads and no tracking companies, and it never sells or shares your information. It is used only to run the yearbook.

It is stored with two well-known services that run the app: Google Firebase (sign-in and the website) and Supabase (the database and photos). Page visits are counted anonymously, with no name, account or IP address, and app errors are reported without your name so they can be fixed.

Who looks after the data?

The yearbook's admin. To keep the yearbook running and safe, the admin can see profile details, including contact details, and keeps a private weekly backup so nothing is lost. The admin's tools never open private letters (unless one is reported), time capsule notes, or who voted for whom.

The admin can give a trusted helper, such as a moderator, access to only the parts of these tools they need, chosen one by one. Everything a helper opens or changes is recorded in an audit log the admin reviews.

The university's Office of the Dean of Students has its own dashboard with the student records it needs (names, registration numbers, programmes, contacts and clubs) and the clubs' activity records. It never sees letters, time capsule notes, votes, friends or comments. Everything it opens or exports is logged.

GLUK's Directorate of External Affairs reads the alumni registration forms students send it, and sees which students of the graduating class have not sent theirs yet, with the contacts on their yearbook profile, so it can remind them. Everything it opens or exports is logged too.

What happens to my alumni registration form?

The alumni registration form, part of your graduation clearance, goes only to GLUK's Directorate of External Affairs and the yearbook's super admin. Other students never see it, and nothing from it appears on the yearbook's pages. It is used for alumni engagement, mentorship, networking and news about the University.

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you can see it and correct it yourself at any time on the form's page, and ask for it to be removed with Withdraw my alumni record: the office then removes it. It is kept apart from your yearbook profile, so deleting your profile does not delete it.

How do I change or delete my information?

Change anything at any time with Edit My Profile on your profile page.

To delete your profile, tap Delete my profile at the bottom of it. It disappears from the yearbook straight away, and for 90 days you can sign in and tap Restore my profile to bring it back. After 90 days it is removed for good, with your photos, comments, likes, friends, letters, time capsule notes and class wall posts. Your alumni registration form is separate (see below).

To have a comment removed, or to ask what is stored about you, email the admin at bandadevelopers2026@gmail.com from the email you signed up with.

Can I turn off notifications?

Yes. Notifications only come if you switch them on. Turn them off any time with Turn off in Alerts, or in your phone's settings.

Still worried about something? Email bandadevelopers2026@gmail.com. Last updated 13 October 2026.
