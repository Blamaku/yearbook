📋 What we collect

Your name, registration number, school, programme, class year, home county, WhatsApp number, email address, bio, hobbies, club memberships, personal memories, and any photos you choose to share.

🎯 How it is used

Your profile will be visible to other GLUK students and alumni on this yearbook platform — solely to celebrate and document your university journey, not for any commercial purpose.

🔐 Your rights

You may edit or delete your profile at any time. You may also choose to submit anonymously — sharing only your name and class details while keeping all other information private.

⚠️ What we don't do

We do not sell your data or share it with third parties outside GLUK. Your WhatsApp number and email are only visible to signed-in users on your profile page.
